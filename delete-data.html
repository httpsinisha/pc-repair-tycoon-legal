Last updated: 2026-11-23

Clear local game data

On Android, open Settings > Apps > PC Repair Tycoon > Storage or Storage & cache > Clear storage (or Clear data). This removes locally saved progress, virtual items and preferences on that device. The wording can vary by phone. Check Android backup settings if you want to avoid restoring app data.

Advertising data

Clearing local game data does not delete information already processed by Google. To manage Google ad privacy or your advertising ID, use Google privacy controls and the controls available in Android settings.

Requests and questions

PC Repair Tycoon has no player account to delete. If you want to request deletion of information you provided directly to support, contact sinisajelisavac5@gmail.com.
