Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Who this applies to

This policy describes how PC Repair Tycoon handles information when you play. For privacy questions or requests, contact sinisajelisavac5@gmail.com.

Progress and settings

Your game saves and settings, including workshop progress, virtual cash, language and sound preferences, are stored in app storage on your device. The game has no player accounts and does not send your workshop save to a game server. Your Android backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that the SDK encrypts this data in transit. Google receives and handles this data according to its privacy policy.

Your choices

The game uses Google User Messaging Platform to check advertising consent. If Google makes privacy options available, open Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings, where supported. Watching an ad is optional.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain in app storage until you clear them or remove the game. If you contact support, we use your message and contact details to reply and retain them as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations. Google manages its advertising data separately under its own policies. See Delete data for the steps to remove local data. You can use the contact above to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal data, as applicable.
